Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

NeverMiss lets people who have saved each other's phone numbers send an urgent alert that can get through silent mode. This policy explains what information we collect, why, who we share it with, and what you can do about it.

Who we are

NeverMiss is run by Nov Sosnowiez, an independent developer in Israel ("we", "us"). We decide how your information is used, which makes us the "controller" under data protection law. You can reach us at info.nevermiss@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Your phone number. We use it to create your account, sign you in, and let your contacts reach you.
  • Your contacts' phone numbers, as codes. With your permission, the app reads the phone numbers in your contacts. It turns each one into a code (a "hash") on your phone before sending it to us. We use these codes only to find which of your contacts also use NeverMiss and have saved you. We never upload contact names, photos or other details. A hash is not encryption, so we protect these codes like the numbers themselves.
  • Emergency contacts. Who invited whom, for which situations, and whether the invite was accepted. If you block someone, we store that too, so they can't invite you again. The person you block isn't told.
  • Alerts. When you send an alert, we store the sender's and recipient's phone numbers, the situation and any detail you chose, the time, and whether the alert was delivered and seen.
  • Your location, for some alerts. When you send a "Not safe", "Home emergency" or "After an attack" alert and you've allowed location access, your phone sends its location with that alert. The person you alert sees it, and we store it with the alert. We never collect your location at any other time, and never in the background.
  • Purchase records. When you pay for an alert, Apple processes the payment. We receive and store Apple's transaction ID so each payment is used only once. We never see your card or Apple account details.
  • Device token. A code from Apple that lets us send notifications to your iPhone.
  • Messages you send us. If you email us or use the Report button (which opens an email), we keep that email and your address so we can reply and act on it.

Health and other sensitive information

Some alert situations, such as "Medical" or "Really bad place", can reveal something about your health or your safety. The law treats this as sensitive information. We use it only to deliver your alert to the person you chose and to show both of you the alert history. By choosing one of these situations and sending the alert, you agree to this use. You can withdraw that consent at any time by not sending these alerts and by deleting your account. Please don't include more personal detail in an alert than the other person needs.

What you must provide, and why

No law requires you to give us information, but NeverMiss can't work without some of it:

  • Phone number: required to create an account.
  • Contacts access: optional. Without it, the app can't find which of your contacts use NeverMiss, so you can't send alerts.
  • Notifications: optional. Without them, you won't receive alerts.
  • Location: optional. Without it, your "Not safe", "Home emergency" and "After an attack" alerts are sent without your location.
  • Name and photo: optional, and they never leave your phone.

What stays on your phone

Your name and profile photo are stored only on your device and are never uploaded. When you receive an alert, your phone looks up the sender's name in your own contacts. That lookup happens on your phone.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your information.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising or tracking tools.
  • We don't use analytics or record how you use the app.
  • We don't use cookies in the app.
  • We don't send marketing messages.
  • We don't make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

Who we share it with

We share information only in these cases:

  • The person you alert or invite. They see your phone number (or their saved name for you), the situation, any detail, and your location when it's included.
  • Service providers. Supabase stores our database and runs our servers. Twilio sends your sign-in code by SMS, so it receives your phone number. Apple delivers notifications and processes payments. They may use your information only to provide their service to us.
  • Legal requirements and safety. We may disclose information when the law requires it, or when we believe in good faith that it's needed to protect someone's safety or to stop fraud or abuse.
  • Professional advisers. Lawyers or accountants, when they need it to advise us, under a duty of confidentiality.
  • A change of ownership. If NeverMiss is sold or transferred, your information may pass to the new owner, who must follow this policy. We'll tell you before that happens.

International transfers

Our database is stored in Ireland, in the European Union. Twilio and Apple may process some information in the United States and other countries. When information leaves the European Economic Area or the UK, we rely on the safeguards the law allows, such as adequacy decisions (Israel has one from the EU) and the providers' standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account exists. When you delete your account, we immediately delete from our database your account, phone number, contact codes, device token, emergency contacts and blocks, and every alert you sent or received, including any location sent with it. Copies may stay in our providers' backups for a short time until they're overwritten. Our providers may also keep their own records, such as SMS logs, for a limited time under their own policies. We keep emails you sent us for as long as we need them to handle your request.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account at any time: Settings → Delete account.
  • Turn off contacts, notifications or location access in iPhone Settings.
  • Leave or block anyone who can alert you, in the app's Contacts tab.
  • Email us to ask for a copy of your data (including in a machine-readable format), to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to how we use it, or to withdraw your consent. We'll reply within 30 days and may ask you to prove the account is yours.
  • Complain to a regulator if you think we've mishandled your information. In Israel this is the Privacy Protection Authority. In the EU, it's the data protection authority where you live. In the UK, it's the Information Commissioner's Office.

Children

NeverMiss is not intended for anyone under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has created an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Each user can read only their own account record, and alerts are visible only to the sender and recipient. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information. If a breach affects you, we'll tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. If the changes are important, we'll tell you in the app before they take effect.
