Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

NeverMiss lets people who have saved each other's phone numbers send an urgent alert that can get through silent mode. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

NeverMiss is run by an independent developer in Israel ("we", "us"). You can reach us at info.nevermiss@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Your phone number. We use it to create your account, sign you in, and let your contacts reach you.
  • Your contacts' phone numbers, as codes. With your permission, the app reads the phone numbers in your contacts. It turns each one into a code (a "hash") on your phone before sending it to us. We use these codes only to find which of your contacts also use NeverMiss and have saved you. We never upload contact names, photos, or other details. A hash is not the same as encryption: someone with enough effort could work out the original number from it, so we protect these codes like the numbers themselves.
  • Emergency contacts. Who invited whom, for which situations, and whether the invite was accepted. If you block someone, we store that too, so they can't invite you again. The person you block isn't told.
  • Alerts. When you send an alert, we store the sender's and recipient's phone numbers, the situation and optional detail you chose, the time, and whether it was delivered and seen.
  • Your location, for some alerts. When you send a "Not safe", "Home emergency" or "After an attack" alert, and you've allowed location access, your phone sends its location with that alert. The person you alert sees it, and we store it with the alert. We never collect your location at any other time, and never in the background.
  • Purchase records. When you pay for an alert, Apple processes the payment. We receive and store Apple's transaction ID so each payment is used only once. We never see your card or Apple account details.
  • Device token. A code from Apple that lets us send notifications to your iPhone.

What you must provide, and why

You don't have to give us any information by law. But NeverMiss can't work without some of it:

  • Phone number: required to create an account. Without it you can't use NeverMiss.
  • Contacts access: optional. Without it, the app can't find which of your contacts use NeverMiss, so you can't send alerts.
  • Notifications: optional. Without them, you won't receive alerts.
  • Location: optional. Without it, your "Not safe", "Home emergency" and "After an attack" alerts are sent without your location.
  • Name and photo: optional, and they never leave your phone.

What stays on your phone

Your name and profile photo are stored only on your device and are never uploaded. When you receive an alert, your phone looks up the sender's name in your own contacts. That lookup happens on your phone.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising or tracking tools.
  • We don't use analytics or record how you use the app.

Services we use

We share information only with the services needed to run NeverMiss:

  • Supabase stores our database and runs our servers.
  • Twilio sends your sign-in code by SMS, so it receives your phone number.
  • Apple delivers notifications and processes payments.

We choose providers that protect your information at least as well as this policy does, and they may use it only to provide their service to us. These providers may store data on servers outside Israel, including in the United States and the European Union. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account exists. When you delete your account, we immediately delete your account, your phone number, your contact codes, your device token, your emergency contacts and blocks, and every alert you sent or received (including any location sent with it) from our database. Our service providers may keep their own records, such as SMS logs, for a limited time under their own policies.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account at any time: Settings → Delete account.
  • Turn off contacts access in iPhone Settings. You won't be able to find contacts to alert until you turn it back on.
  • Turn off notifications in iPhone Settings. You won't receive alerts.
  • Turn off location access in iPhone Settings. Your alerts will be sent without your location.
  • Leave or block anyone who can alert you, in the app's Contacts tab.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct it, by email. We'll reply within 30 days.
  • Complain to Israel's Privacy Protection Authority if you think we've mishandled your information.

Children

NeverMiss is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has created an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Each user can read only their own account record, and alerts are visible only to the sender and recipient. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. If we make important changes, we'll tell you in the app.
