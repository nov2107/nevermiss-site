Questions

Doesn’t my iPhone already do this?

Partly. iPhone’s Emergency Bypass makes a person’s calls and texts ring through silent. But it’s every call, including the ones that can wait, which makes it hard to leave on. And the person calling can’t tell if you have. NeverMiss only rings when someone taps “I need you”, so you can leave it on for the people who matter. They see where you are, and you know when they’ve heard it.

Does it ring when their phone is on silent?

Yes. It rings like an iPhone alarm, even on silent and Do Not Disturb, once they’ve allowed alarms for NeverMiss. It needs iOS 26 or later.

Is it free?

Yes. Downloading NeverMiss and sending alerts is free, with no ads.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. NeverMiss is for iPhone, and the people you alert need it on their iPhone too.

Who sees my location?

Only the person you alert, and only if you’ve allowed location. It’s sent with the alert and never collected at any other time. Privacy policy

What if I send one by mistake?

You get three seconds to cancel before it goes. If it’s already sent, stop it from the app and tell them why. They get that straight away.

NeverMiss is not an emergency service
