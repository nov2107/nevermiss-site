Common questions

Why can't I send an alert to someone?

You can only alert people who have NeverMiss and who have saved your phone number in their contacts, and whom you have saved too. Ask them to open NeverMiss once so their contacts are up to date.

I paid but the alert didn't arrive.

If delivery fails, the app lets you try again without paying a second time. If that doesn't work, email us with the time you sent it. Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I didn't get my sign-in code by SMS.

Check the number and country code, wait a minute, and try again. Some carriers delay or block these messages. If it keeps happening, email us and tell us your carrier.

How do I stop someone from alerting me?

Block them in the app, or remove their number from your contacts.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings and tap Delete account. Your account and data are removed right away. See our Privacy Policy for details.
