Most people keep Do Not Disturb on at night. So the one call that really matters can go unanswered until morning.

How it works

  1. 1Pick what’s happening

    Medical, not safe, home emergency, or after an attack. No typing.

  2. 2Pick who

    Only people who agreed to that situation show up. Add a detail if you have a second. Your location goes with it when it helps.

  3. 3Know it reached them

    It gets through their Do Not Disturb and Focus, and rings again if they don’t open it. You see when they’ve seen it, and they can answer “On my way”.

Questions

Doesn’t my iPhone already do this?

Partly. iPhone’s Emergency Bypass lets one contact’s calls and texts ring through, even on silent. It’s worth turning on. But a call needs you to talk, and a text needs you to type. NeverMiss is for when you can’t: two taps tell them what’s wrong and where you are, and you know when they’ve seen it.

Does it ring when their phone is on silent?

Not yet. It rings through Do Not Disturb and Focus. On silent mode, it still shows up on their screen, without sound. I’ve asked Apple for permission to ring through silent too.

Is it free?

Yes. Downloading NeverMiss and sending alerts is free, with no ads.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. NeverMiss is for iPhone, and the people you alert need it on their iPhone too.

Who sees my location?

Only the person you alert, and only with a “Not safe”, “Home emergency” or “After an attack” alert, if you’ve allowed location. It’s never collected at any other time. Privacy policy

What if I send one by mistake?

You get three seconds to cancel before it goes. If it’s already sent, end it from the app and tell them it was a mistake. They get that straight away.
