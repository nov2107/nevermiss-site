When it can’t wait, it gets through.

NeverMiss sends an urgent alert to the few people you choose. It rings even on silent and Do Not Disturb, so no one is left in trouble with no one to reach.

One email when NeverMiss launches. Nothing else.

How it works

  1. 1

    Choose your people

    Invite the few people you’d want in an emergency. Each of them accepts once, for the situations they agree to.

  2. 2

    Say what’s happening

    Medical emergency, not safe, home emergency, a really bad place, or after an attack. Two taps, no typing.

  3. 3

    Their phone rings

    Even on silent and Do Not Disturb. When it matters, they also see where you are, so they can call back or come.

Only for real emergencies

A sound that gets through silent is only worth something if nobody abuses it. So NeverMiss is strict.

  • Five situations, nothing else

    There’s no message box. Nobody can use it for “call me back” or “where are you?”.

  • They say yes first

    You can only alert someone who accepted you, and only for the situations they chose.

  • Leave or block any time

    Anyone can stop alerts from a person with one tap, and block them from inviting again.

Why I built NeverMiss

One night, something happened near my home, the kind of moment where you need your family right away. I called, and nobody answered. Their phones were on silent, like most people’s are at night.

Silent mode did exactly what it’s meant to do: it kept everyone out, including me. I built NeverMiss so the few people who matter most can always get through, but only when it really counts.

Nov, founder
